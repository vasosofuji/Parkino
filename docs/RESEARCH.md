## Public parking sources

1. [Gradski tariff page](https://www.gradskiparking.com.mk/javni-parkiralishta-i-zonsko.nspx) distinguishes zoned street parking from barrier-controlled public lots. Baseline zone rates are A: 40, B: 30, C/D: 25 MKD/hour; A0 first hour 75, later hours 50. A has a two-hour limit; B four hours. Do not apply zone rates to every lot with a similar name.
2. [Parking.MK Skopje inventory](https://parking.mk/?language=en&page=static&section=town_skopje) provides the 35 codes included in this project's inventory. Its text list is insufficient to locate every sign precisely. The site also documents schedules and exceptional surcharges; these are not implemented as definitive payment quotes.
3. [Gradski garage page](https://cms.gradskiparking.com.mk/uslugi-katna-garaza.nspx) identifies Beko and 26 July with capacities 605 and 445; the operator's [garage price list](https://www.gradskiparking.com.mk/cenovnik-katna-garaza.nspx) lists both garages at 30 MKD/hour. OSM feature IDs were matched by name and capacity.
4. [POC homepage](https://poc.mk/) lists sector rates 0: 100, 1: 70, 2: 60 MKD/hour. It lists Dom na Gradezhnici at 250 MKD/hour. Its 50-MKD Judicial Palace listing was not automatically assigned to the Judicial Palace garage because the operator distinguishes the facility types.
5. [POC zone 0](https://poc.mk/?page_id=165), [zone 1](https://poc.mk/?page_id=167), [zone 2](https://poc.mk/?page_id=169) link the publicly downloadable maps used here. They supplied 13 sector polygons, not every individual parking bay or sign. Exact operating restrictions and resident-only streets require additional verification.
6. [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) supplies community-maintained feature geometry under ODbL. The bounding-box query used `amenity=parking`, coordinates 41.91/21.30 to 42.08/21.58, returning 867 records. It yielded 866 importable coordinates. This is not a boundary-accurate official municipal register, and it can omit street segments, new facilities and untagged spaces.
7. [ADSDP government garages](https://adsdp.mk/pages/катни-гаражи/) appear in OSM operator/website tags and represent another authority to investigate. Their tariffs were not inferred from Gradski prices.

## Coverage audit

The map includes 866 OSM records and 13 POC sector polygons. Thirty-five Gradski sign codes remain in the inventory pending exact geometry. Only three individual facilities have matched official tariffs. The six bundled destination landmarks support local search; any other destination can be chosen directly on the map. No fake current occupancy or unsupported zone A42 record is seeded.

The next data task is an operator-by-operator register: Gradski, POC, ADSDP, commercial garages/malls, and other municipalities/owners. Obtain entrances, individual sign codes, legal boundaries, effective tariff rules, payment methods, operating hours, resident/customer restrictions, accessible spaces and capacities. Audit signs on the ground and reconcile overlapping OSM features. Avoid treating an entire tariff polygon as a legal parking area.

## Government collaboration

[Municipality Centar's 7 July 2026 announcement](https://www.centar.gov.mk/en/node/2690) advertises digital parking services and sensor plans. This is evidence of relevant initiatives, not a verified public occupancy API. The app therefore implements an authenticated ingestion contract without claiming a government connection. Gradski's [public-information page](https://www.gradskiparking.com.mk/public-information.nspx) provides another official route for requesting the static register.

A sensible pilot is one garage with a known entrance and dependable free-space count. Validate feed timestamps, freshness, total capacity and correction behaviour before expanding. See the implementation contract in [GOVERNMENT-INTEGRATION.md](GOVERNMENT-INTEGRATION.md). No officials or companies were contacted during this task.
